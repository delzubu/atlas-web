# Languages

Use Languages to practise a language pair in short sessions. An agent can build lessons from your notes. Choose lessons and question formats, see the correct answer after each question, and track your results and streak.

## How to use Languages

1. Open a language pair and choose one or more lessons.
2. Start a practice session and choose the level, exercise formats and question count. Work through the questions and review the answer after each one.
3. Review the result, revisit weak items and track progress over time.

A pair has a learning direction, such as German learned from English. An agent can turn your notes into lesson items, examples and exercises, and score translations submitted for review.
