# Languages

Use Languages to practise a language pair in short sessions. An agent can build lessons from your notes. Choose lessons and question formats, see the correct answer after each question, and track your results and streak.

## How to use Languages

1. Open a language pair and choose one or more lessons.
2. Start a practice session and choose the level, exercise formats and question count. Work through the questions and review the answer after each one.
3. Review the result, revisit weak items and track progress over time.

A pair has a learning direction, such as German learned from English. An agent can turn your notes into lesson items, examples and exercises, and score translations submitted for review.

## Practise a group of lessons

Practice requires reviewer or higher. Reader access lets you read lessons and existing results; it does not offer practice, retake or cancellation actions. Writers can also author lessons.


Practise starts from the lessons you ticked. Choose:

- **Level.** *Easy* only offers choices; *Normal* adds typing; *Hard* adds typing and sentences to translate.
- **Exercise types.** Switch any off.
- **Questions.** From 6 to 30.

Your choices are remembered for next time. Then **Start**.

### One question at a time

The question fills the screen. **Check** scores your answer at once and shows the right answer and why; the question is then locked. **Next** goes on, **Skip** leaves a question unanswered.
On a keyboard, **Enter** checks or goes on and **1–9** choose an option. A typed answer is scored kindly: a missing umlaut, a capital letter, an article or one slip in spelling earns half the points and
tells you the fix. Grammar forms and articles are strict. Use the character bar for letters your keyboard lacks.

A gap or a grammar exercise shows what the sentence means in your own language, on the line under it, so you know which word is meant; you still have to find
the word itself. Only the lesson's word counts, plus any other words the lesson lists as also right: another word with the same meaning is marked wrong, and the
correction tells you which word the lesson practises.

A sentence to **translate** is not checked here. An agent scores it after you finish, so the result is *pending* for a while (reading a result).

Your answers are saved as you go. Leave with the **×** and choose **Continue** on the start screen to carry on.

### Finishing

The last question leads to *Ready to finish?*. Unanswered questions score nothing, and you cannot change answers afterwards.
