# NPC

Use NPC to keep character and group sheets for a Dungeons & Dragons campaign. Maintain a canon register and campaign versions, record what happens in play, and track relationships, plans, and progress clocks.

## How to use NPC

1. Open your campaign register. Create a person or group, or import a canon entry as of an in-world date.
2. Use the sheet to edit sections such as description, history, statistics, relationships and plans. The campaign version can evolve alongside the canon entry.
3. After a session, record appearances and changes, or advance plans and faction clocks between sessions. Use the register views to inspect the cast and its relationships.

NPC is designed for the game master. It supports named stat-block versions, campaign history, canon refresh and publish, and optional tracking of friendship with player characters.
