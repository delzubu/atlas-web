# Oppos

Oppos helps authorised teams review sales opportunities, compare changes over time, and track the board tasks associated with those opportunities.

## How to use Oppos

1. Use **Overview** to review opportunities by stage and **Changes** to compare updates since a date.
2. Open an opportunity to inspect its details. Use **Board** to follow linked tasks or **Review** to work through them in a meeting.
3. Connect your Microsoft account for live board access. With board edit access, update tasks or link them to the relevant opportunity.

Oppos brings CRM opportunity information and Microsoft Planner board tasks into the same workflow. Board edits are made in Microsoft Planner with your own account.

## Link a delivery task to an opportunity

**Overview** includes unlinked delivery tasks by default. Use **Include delivery tasks** to control their visibility. With edit access and a connected Microsoft account, drag a task to an opportunity or choose **Link to an opportunity…** from the task’s menu. The task’s Oppos notes move to the opportunity’s **Comments** tab and return to the task on unlink.

Reader and reviewer access is scoped to accounts assigned to the person. Agent clients use the Oppos MCP v2 interface.
