# Study

Use Study to learn from a collection of source documents. An agent can prepare readings, guiding questions, and tests from the collection. Work through the material in the browser, review feedback on your answers, and track your progress.

## How to use Study

1. Open a corpus and choose a learning path that fits your goal. Read the linked sources and work through the questions and perspectives in the path.
2. Open a test when you are ready. Reader access allows you to read the path. Reviewer access or higher lets you start a test.
3. Review results and feedback after scoring. Written answers can remain pending until an agent grades them.

A corpus keeps source material together and can support multiple independent learning paths. Each path has its own scope, difficulty, tests and scores.
