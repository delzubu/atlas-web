# Study

Use Study to learn from a collection of source documents. An agent can prepare readings, guiding questions, and tests from the collection. Work through the material in the browser, review feedback on your answers, and track your progress.

## How to use Study

1. Open a corpus and choose a learning path that fits your goal. Read the linked sources and work through the questions and perspectives in the path.
2. Open a test when you are ready. Reader access allows you to read the path. Reviewer access or higher lets you start a test.
3. Review results and feedback after scoring. Written answers can remain pending until an agent grades them.

A corpus keeps source material together and can support multiple independent learning paths. Each path has its own scope, difficulty, tests and scores.

## Take and submit a test

Start a test from its path. Your answers are saved as you go, so you can leave and come back: an unfinished test appears under
**Your attempts**.

### Pick a level

Before you start, pick a **level**. The path suggests one, and you can change it.

- **Easy**: ten questions, mostly single choices and short fill-ins, and a few just-for-fun questions on paths that have them.
- **Normal**: ten questions, the usual mix, and a very few just-for-fun questions.
- **Hard**: fifteen questions, up to two written questions, and no jokes.

Written questions are only in **Hard** unless the corpus or its workspace allows them at other levels (settings).
Where a level has one, it has one written question (two on Hard).

Your results are kept **per level**. Full marks on easy is not the same as full marks on hard, so every result shows its level, and
only normal and hard results count towards understanding shown.

### Pictures in questions

A question may include a picture (a photo, a map, a diagram). It is shown with the question and in the review, as it was when
you took the test. Pictures are shown as thumbnails, side by side where there is room, so the question stays in view (a small copy is loaded, which is quick): **click a picture to enlarge it** (the full picture is then loaded), and press Esc or click outside to close it. If a picture cannot be loaded, the question says so; the question can still be answered from the text.

### Which questions you get

A test holds more questions than one sitting asks. Each attempt is a random selection from it, so taking a test again asks different
questions. The questions come in random order, and the written question (two on hard, where a level has them) is always last. The answers you choose from are
shuffled too.

### One question at a time

You see one question at a time, and the whole test fits the window: the numbered bar and the buttons stay in place, and only a long
question scrolls, so the question and its answers stay together. Use **Back** and **Next**, or the numbered bar to jump to any question;
a ✓ marks a question that is answered. Going back never loses an answer.

- **Flag for review** marks a question you want to come back to (⚑ in the bar and in the review). Flags are only for you; they are not saved.
- Keys: **Alt+←** and **Alt+→** turn the page; **1–9** choose that option of a choice question (press again to untick on a multiple choice).
- The line under the title shows how many are answered and whether your answers are saved. If saving fails it says **not saved**: use
  **Retry**. Closing the tab with unsaved answers asks first.

### Question types

- **Choose one**: four answers, one is right.
- **Choose several**: tick every answer that is right. There is more than one, and a wrong tick takes away from a right one.
- **Match**: drag each item from the pool below onto the row it belongs to. Drop an item on a filled row to swap it in, or
  back on the pool to take it out.
- **Fill in**: drag each word from the pool into the blank it completes.
- **Written**: explain in your own words, and say what in the sources supports it. There is often more than one good answer.
- **Just for fun**: marked with a badge. Any answer counts. A written one is read for how funny and creative it is, and it always earns full points.

Without a mouse, or on a touch screen: select an item (tap, or press Enter), then select the place for it. **Delete** on a
placed item takes it back out.

### Submitting

**Review and submit** is on the last question (or choose **Finish…** earlier). The review lists every question as answered or not,
and flagged ones; click a question to go back to it. **Submit** is there, and it says how many questions are unanswered (they score
nothing). You cannot change answers afterwards. Choice, match and fill-in questions are scored **at once**.
Written answers are sent to be scored (see waiting for a score).

Each time you start a test you get a new attempt. Earlier attempts are kept and never overwritten.
