# Articles

Atlas — The Light Factory helps you explore, design and refine work that does not come with a finished blueprint. These articles explain practical ways to co-create with AI while keeping your work in a shared environment, independent of any particular model provider.

## The co-creation workflow

- [A practical workflow for co-creation]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link docs/articles/structured-co-creation.md %}) — Separate inputs, interpretation, research, modelling and rendering, with review throughout.
- [Creating a technical presentation with AI]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link docs/articles/technical-presentation.md %}) — Apply the method to an Atlas presentation, from audience and evidence to a reviewed storyline and slides.

## Co-creating with AI

Follow these six articles in order, or start with the topic you need. A fictional local-history exhibition provides a continuous worked example.

1. [Working together in a shared workspace]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link docs/articles/co-creating/shared-workspace.md %}) — Work on shared artifacts and review changes with an agent of your choice.
2. [Continuing work across conversations and agents]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link docs/articles/co-creating/continuing-work.md %}) — Preserve context and continue with another agent or subscription.
3. [Working with documents beyond Markdown]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link docs/articles/co-creating/working-with-documents.md %}) — Import source documents and make their content accessible.
4. [Organising complex documents into bundles]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link docs/articles/co-creating/bundles.md %}) — Present multiple authored files as one coherent artifact.
5. [Going beyond documents]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link docs/articles/co-creating/beyond-documents.md %}) — Choose slides, knowledge bases, plans and models to fit the task.
6. [From raw material to finished work]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link docs/articles/co-creating/from-inputs-to-output.md %}) — Interpret inputs, research, model, review and render the result.
