# Playbooks

A playbook guides you and an agent through a repeatable piece of work, such as setting up a knowledge base, turning sources into study material or preparing a proposal. It gives the agent a workflow and describes the inputs and expected outputs. Atlas apps provide the tools the agent uses to read, create and revise the work.

## Use a playbook

1. Open the relevant Atlas workspace and connect an agent with access to it and the required apps.
2. Ask for the outcome you want, or name a playbook from this guide. Share the workspace, source files and any constraints that matter.
3. Review the agent’s questions and proposals as you work. Inspect the resulting files, plans or records in the relevant app.

The agent can find available playbooks through Workspace and open them by their `atlas://playbook/<id>` reference. Your instance’s library determines which playbooks and templates are available.
