These are suggested combinations, not a single enforced workflow. Choose only the methods that suit your task and available apps.

## Use a playbook

1. Open a project and connect an agent with access to it and the required tools.
2. Ask for the outcome, or name a playbook and provide the inputs and constraints.
3. Inspect intermediate results, respond to questions and refine the work where needed.

Agents can discover available playbooks through Workspace and open them by their `atlas://playbook/<id>` references. Your instance's configured library determines what is available. A playbook guides an agent; it is not a guarantee of identical execution across models.

For an example of several methods working together, see [Creating a technical presentation with AI]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link docs/articles/technical-presentation.md %}).
