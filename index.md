## Why Atlas?

**Industrial power. Bespoke thinking.**

Some work can be specified up front and delegated end to end. Other work — research, design, planning, learning and creative projects — evolves as you discover what matters. Atlas is designed for the latter, without giving up autonomous execution of well-understood tasks.

| Choose your AI | Keep your work | Use the right representation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Continue with another compatible agent when the task or your subscription changes. | Files, revisions, review comments and working instructions remain in the shared project. | Work with plans, models, maps and knowledge structures as well as documents. |

Unlike keeping the working artifact solely inside a provider's chat or artifact environment, Atlas separates the shared work from the choice of agent. Connections use MCP. Each client still has its own capabilities, subscription, privacy terms and usage limits.

## A working method, not a rigid process

Give an agent the context, inspect its draft and refine it together. For substantial work, separate input interpretation, research, modelling and the final presentation. Keep each intermediate result accessible for review. Atlas lets you alternate between co-creation, independent delegation and expert judgement.
