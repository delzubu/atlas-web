Atlas brings industrial-scale AI capability to work that does not come with a finished blueprint. It equips experts with agentic tools to explore, design, build, evaluate and refine unique outcomes, even when the requirements are incomplete or change along the way.

Atlas is a suite of connected knowledge-work tools. You use them in the browser, and agents use the same tools over MCP.
